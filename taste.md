# Product Taste

## Overall Vibe
- **Minimalist**: Clean lines, uncluttered interface.
- **Friendly**: Approachable language, soft colors.
- **Efficient**: Quick task entry and completion.

## User Experience Goals
- **Speed**: Add a task in under 3 seconds.
- **Clarity**: Immediate visual feedback on task completion.
- **Joy**: Small animations (e.g., task fade-out) feel satisfying.

## Tone of Voice
- Positive, encouraging, concise.
- Use active verbs: "Add task", "Mark done", "Remove".

## Key Interactions
- **Drag-and-drop reordering** (optional).
- **Swipe to delete** on mobile.
- **Keyboard shortcuts**: `Ctrl+Enter` to add, `Enter` to edit.

## Accessibility Notes
- Focus indicators visible.
- ARIA labels for button actions.
- Color contrast meets WCAG AA.
